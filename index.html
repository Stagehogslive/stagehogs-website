Meet the band

FOUR FRIENDS.
ONE LOUD HISTORY.

Shane Berg, Miles McGillivray, Tyler Murphy, and Tyler Keezer started playing together in a high-school punk band. Careers and families followed, but the music never went away.

Now back together as The StageHogs, they bring 90s alternative, 2000s rock, punk favorites and reimagined classics to the stage. No throwaway songs. No going through the motions.
